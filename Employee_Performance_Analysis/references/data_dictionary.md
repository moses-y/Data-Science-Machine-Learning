# Employee Data Dictionary

This data dictionary provides detailed descriptions of each attribute in the Employee Performance dataset used at INX Future Inc.

- **EmpNumber**: Unique identifier for each employee.
- **Age**: Age of the employee in years.
- **Gender**: Gender of the employee (Male, Female).
- **EducationBackground**: Academic background of the employee (e.g., Life Sciences, Medical, Marketing).
- **MaritalStatus**: Marital status of the employee (Single, Married, Divorced).
- **EmpDepartment**: Department in which the employee works (e.g., Sales, Research & Development, Human Resources).
- **EmpJobRole**: Specific role of the employee within the department (e.g., Sales Executive, Laboratory Technician).
- **BusinessTravelFrequency**: Frequency of travel for business (Frequently, Rarely, None).
- **DistanceFromHome**: Distance from work to home in kilometers.
- **EmpEducationLevel**: Level of education completed by the employee.
- **EmpEnvironmentSatisfaction**: Satisfaction with the work environment, rated on a scale from 1 to 4.
- **EmpHourlyRate**: Hourly rate of the employee in USD.
- **EmpJobInvolvement**: Level of involvement in the job, rated on a scale from 1 to 4.
- **EmpJobLevel**: Level of the job held in the organizational hierarchy.
- **EmpJobSatisfaction**: Satisfaction with the job, rated on a scale from 1 to 4.
- **NumCompaniesWorked**: Number of companies the employee has worked at.
- **OverTime**: Whether the employee has overtime (Yes, No).
- **EmpLastSalaryHikePercent**: The percentage of the most recent salary hike.
- **EmpRelationshipSatisfaction**: Satisfaction with interpersonal relationships at work, rated on a scale from 1 to 4.
- **TotalWorkExperienceInYears**: Total years of professional work experience.
- **TrainingTimesLastYear**: Number of times the employee received training last year.
- **EmpWorkLifeBalance**: Rating of work-life balance, from 1 (poor) to 4 (excellent).
- **ExperienceYearsAtThisCompany**: Total number of years the employee has worked at INX Future Inc.
- **ExperienceYearsInCurrentRole**: Number of years the employee has been in their current role.
- **YearsSinceLastPromotion**: Number of years since the employee's last promotion.
- **YearsWithCurrManager**: Number of years the employee has been with their current manager.
- **Attrition**: Whether the employee has left the company (Yes, No).
- **PerformanceRating**: Performance rating of the employee, typically on a scale from 1 to 4.
