# Model Performance Reports

## Overview
This document presents the performance metrics for the models used in the Employee Performance Analysis at INX Future Inc. It includes evaluations based on accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 scores, as well as cross-validation results.

## Model Evaluations
- **Logistic Regression**: 
  - Accuracy: 84.58%
  - Precision: 84.78%
  - Recall: 84.58%
  - F1 Score: 84.53%
- **Random Forest**:
  - Accuracy: 92.92%
  - Precision: 93.20%
  - Recall: 92.92%
  - F1 Score: 92.52%
- **Gradient Boosting**:
  - Accuracy: 92.50%
  - Precision: 92.46%
  - Recall: 92.50%
  - F1 Score: 92.41%

## Cross-Validation Results
- Average CV Score for Gradient Boosting: 93.49%

## Conclusion
The detailed performance metrics and cross-validation scores highlight the effectiveness and reliability of the models deployed. Gradient Boosting emerged as the top performer, offering the best balance of accuracy and complexity.

These reports will aid in understanding the strengths and limitations of each model, providing a basis for future improvements and refinements.
