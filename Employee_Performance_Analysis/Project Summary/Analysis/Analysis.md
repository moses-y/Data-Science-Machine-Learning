### Analysis.md
This document details the methodologies and analytical processes employed throughout the project, from data preprocessing to model development and evaluation.

**Data Processing and Exploratory Analysis**

Data preprocessing involved meticulous cleaning, normalization, and encoding to prepare the dataset for analysis, detailed in `data_processing.ipynb`. Exploratory analysis conducted in `data_exploratory_analysis.ipynb` utilized statistical tests and visualizations to understand data distributions and relationships, informing subsequent modeling decisions.

**Model Development and Insights**

Models including Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting were developed and compared in `train_model.ipynb`. Gradient Boosting emerged as the most effective model, with a detailed feature importance analysis highlighting factors like Employee Environment Satisfaction and Salary Hike Percent as pivotal.

**Cross-Validation and Model Selection**

Robust cross-validation techniques affirmed the model's efficacy, achieving an average CV score of 93.5%, indicating high reliability and predictive power across varied data subsets.
