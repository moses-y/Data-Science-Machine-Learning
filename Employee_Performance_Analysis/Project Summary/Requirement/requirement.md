### Requirement.md

**Project Scope**

The Employee Performance Analysis project at INX Future Inc was initiated to identify key factors influencing employee performance and propose data-driven strategies to enhance productivity and satisfaction. 
Key deliverables were identified as follows:

Development of predictive models to assess factors influencing employee performance.
Identification of top factors affecting employee performance through feature importance analysis.
Recommendations for improving employee performance based on analytical findings.

**Data Requirements**

The data utilized encompassed a range of employee attributes, including but not limited to age, department, job roles, satisfaction levels, salary hikes, years since last promotion, and work-life balance. Data integrity and preprocessing were paramount to ensure accuracy and relevance of the analysis.

**Sources and Compliance**

Data were sourced from internal company records, ensuring adherence to ethical guidelines and data privacy laws. Special attention was given to maintaining data integrity and confidentiality throughout the project's lifecycle.

**Objectives**

The main objectives were:
- To develop predictive models to determine the influence of various factors on employee performance.
- To identify critical performance determinants through data analysis.
- To offer actionable recommendations aimed at improving performance metrics across the company.
